How to Embed in Notion

  1. Host this file or upload it to GitHub Pages / Vercel / Netlify to get a direct web link (URL).
  2. Open your page or database inside Notion.
  3. Type /embed and hit Enter.
  4. Paste the URL of your hosted transposer app and click Embed Link.
  5. Drag the bottom corner handle in Notion to expand the frame size.
Once embedded, your chord transposer works continuously inside your Notion songbook workspace!
